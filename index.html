A little about us

Growing a greener, more beautiful community.

The garden is more than a place. It is a way to connect, learn, create and give back.

The Bartram Garden Club, Inc. was chartered in September 2016. Our mission is to promote the love of gardening, floral design, civic beautification and environmental responsibility in our community.

We are proud to serve Amelia Island and the Nassau County area, bringing people together through hands-on projects, educational programs and a shared appreciation for the natural world.
